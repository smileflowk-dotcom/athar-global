## Individual qualification decision (same for every one of 20)

Every candidate starts as **READY_TO_REQUEST_LAB_FEASIBILITY_REVIEW**. This is NOT experimental sample readiness.

1. **EXTERNAL_REVIEW**: a named researcher or qualified material supplier reviews whether the target counterion form and analytical assay are chemically meaningful. Save *actual* reply text/date; do not infer approval from no reply.
2. **PRODUCT**: verify manufacturer, grade, resin functional group (Type I vs Type II), chloride or other shipped form, batch/COA/SDS, availability and actual supplier.
3. **ION_LOADING**: lab validates a feasible preparation route and intended salt/protonation state (sulfite oxidation, phosphate pH speciation, borate chemistry and multivalent exchange). Quantitatively verify post-exchange identity, capacity and stability. An exchanged material **cannot** be called ready if this evidence is missing.
4. **MEASUREMENT**: lab confirms equipment for **CO₂-specific** uptake/desorption, RH-controlled and temperature-matched measurements, dry-mass/site-normalized working capacity, kinetics, repeated cycles, and same-resin carbonate/bicarbonate + blanks.
5. **SCIENTIFIC_GATES**: retain A/B/C/D/E statuses from V4; a manufacturer's supply confirmation or a lab's willingness to test does not override unresolved strict ML, paper review, atomistic GFN1, structure transfer issues. Such a candidate may still be tested *as falsification/exploration*, explicitly marked as such.

**GO to physical experiment scheduling** only when a named laboratory has agreed to make/obtain an identified batch, establish the exchange/identity measurement plan, approve safety and scientific design, and agreed test resources/budget with project owner. Anything less is still a well-defined proposal, not a laboratory-verified test-ready sample.
